Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

TL;DR: We collect minimal data, use it to provide AI-powered study features, and never sell your information. Your study materials stay private and secure.

1. Introduction

Welcome to StudyMate AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our AI-powered learning platform.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, profile picture (if using Google Sign-In)
  • Study Content: Notes, PDFs, images, audio recordings, and other materials you upload
  • User-Generated Content: Questions you ask, summaries you create, quiz responses, flashcards
  • Settings & Preferences: AI provider choice, theme preferences, notification settings

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Features used, time spent, study sessions, interaction patterns
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type, screen resolution
  • Performance Data: Crash reports, error logs (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (via Firebase Analytics)

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Sign-In: Basic profile information (name, email, profile picture)
  • AI Providers: We process your queries through third-party AI services (Gemini, OpenAI, etc.) according to their terms

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide Core Services: AI chat, quiz generation, flashcards, summaries, weak topic detection
  • Personalization: Tailor learning recommendations and study plans to your needs
  • Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve features and fix bugs
  • Communication: Send important updates, study reminders (if enabled), and support responses
  • Security: Detect fraud, prevent abuse, and protect user accounts
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations

4. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

Your study content may be processed by third-party AI providers you select:

  • Google Gemini: Subject to Google's Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI (GPT models): Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy
  • xAI (Grok): Subject to xAI's privacy terms
  • OpenRouter: Subject to OpenRouter's privacy policy
  • Ollama (Local): Processed entirely on your device (no data sent externally)

Important: When using cloud AI providers, your queries and uploaded content are sent to their servers for processing. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

5. Data Storage & Security

5.1 Where We Store Data

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): User accounts, cloud sync data, uploaded files
  • Local Device Storage: SQLite database (via Drift) for offline access
  • Secure Storage: API keys encrypted locally using flutter_secure_storage

5.2 Security Measures

  • End-to-end encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firebase Authentication for secure login
  • Encrypted API key storage on device
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access controls and permission systems

Note: While we implement industry-standard security practices, no system is 100% secure. Use strong passwords and keep your account credentials private.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal data. We may share data only in these limited cases:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing
  • Service Providers: Firebase, AI providers, analytics tools (bound by confidentiality agreements)
  • Legal Requirements: Court orders, legal processes, or to prevent harm
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale (users will be notified)

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-Out: Disable analytics, notifications, or data collection features
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions previously granted

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@studymateai.app

8. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained as long as your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Data permanently deleted within 30 days (except legal holds)
  • Backups: May persist in backups for up to 90 days
  • Anonymous Analytics: May be retained indefinitely in aggregated form

9. Children's Privacy

StudyMate AI is intended for users 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us immediately.

10. International Users

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your jurisdiction (primarily United States, where Firebase/Google Cloud operates). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.

11. Cookies & Tracking

We use:

  • Essential Cookies: For authentication and core functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage tracking)
  • Local Storage: To cache app state and enable offline mode

You can disable non-essential cookies in Settings, though some features may be limited.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
